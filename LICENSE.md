## Why ODbL?

This dataset combines data from two source licenses:

| Source | Provider | License |
|---|---|---|
| Fountains, libraries, churches, pools | Stadt Graz OGD / Grazer Freizeitbetriebe | CC BY 4.0 |
| Parks and green spaces | OpenStreetMap contributors | ODbL 1.0 |

Because OpenStreetMap data is licensed under ODbL with a **share-alike** clause for derivative databases, the combined dataset must be released under ODbL 1.0. This is the most restrictive compatible license that covers all inputs and satisfies the share-alike requirement.
